# Haven benchmark results

660 fictional clinical notes in 11 styles, generated by `benchmark/notes.py` (seed 2026). Re-run with `python -m benchmark.run`.

These notes were written by the same people who wrote Haven's recognisers, so treat the numbers as an upper bound. Real notes are messier.

## Summary

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Identifiers caught (standard cases) | 100.0% (2354/2354) |
| Identifiers caught (hard cases) | 82.5% (99/120) |
| Clinical phrases left intact | 99.9% (1465/1466) |
| Average time per note | 14 ms |

For comparison, Presidio on its own (default settings, same notes) caught 80.1% of standard identifiers and left 71.6% of clinical phrases intact. The difference is Haven's own recognisers.

Hard cases are names that are also ordinary words (Grace Hill, Will Bell) and names typed in lower case.
